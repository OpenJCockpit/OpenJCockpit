# reporting-dashboard.spec.md

## Business intent

Managers and HR must have real-time insight into absenteeism and leave figures per department,
exportable to the organization's reporting standard.

## Acceptance criteria

- The dashboard shows current absenteeism figures at department level.
- Leave planning is visible as a team calendar per quarter.
- Reports can be exported as CSV and PDF.
- Historical data is available for up to 3 years back.
